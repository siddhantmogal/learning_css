Playing outside offers many important benefits for your physical and mental health. When you spend time outdoors, your body absorbs natural sunlight which helps create Vitamin D. This vitamin keeps your bones strong and supports your immune system. Fresh air and physical activity like running or walking also boost your mood and lower your stress levels. To sum up, taking a short break outside every day is a simple way to feel happier and healthier.
